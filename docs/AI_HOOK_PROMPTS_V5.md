Lanjutan [v1](./AI_HOOK_PROMPTS_V1.md) · [v2](./AI_HOOK_PROMPTS_V2.md) · [v3](./AI_HOOK_PROMPTS_V3.md) · [v4](./AI_HOOK_PROMPTS_V4.md).

## Pengakuan: 20 ide sebelumnya salah arah

Transkrip video referensi menjelaskan kenapa tidak ada yang tembus 5.

Video yang dapat **25 juta views** — 200x lipat konten biasa kreator itu — isinya
cuma **mangkuk yang tergelincir**. Bukan ledakan, bukan gravitasi mati, bukan
produk raksasa. Mangkuk. Tergelincir.

> *"Your brain is always trying to guess what happens next. But the second that
> bowl slipped, that guess broke. Suddenly people needed to know — did it break?
> What's her reaction going to be? … Not better lighting, not better editing.
> Just one moment that breaks what the viewer expected."*

Semua ide saya dari Ide 1 sampai 20 adalah **spektakel yang dirancang**. Dan
justru itu masalahnya: spektakel **terlihat dibuat**. Otak penonton tidak
bertanya "apa yang terjadi selanjutnya?", tapi langsung menyimpulkan "ini
efek". Tidak ada tebakan yang patah, karena tidak ada tebakan yang dibangun.

Mangkuk tergelincir bekerja justru karena **terlihat seperti kecelakaan sungguhan**.

Ini juga menjelaskan papan nilainya: Ide 8 (pasar beku) dapat nilai tertinggi
4.8 karena itu **satu-satunya ide yang setiap elemennya masih terlihat seperti
rekaman nyata**. Yang paling banyak efeknya justru yang gagal.

---

## Tiga aturan baru dari transkrip

**Aturan F — Pattern interrupt harus terlihat KECELAKAAN, bukan spektakel.**
Makin terlihat "dirancang", makin lemah. Target barunya bukan "wah, keren",
tapi **"eh, itu tadi kenapa?"**

> Koreksi untuk Ide 16 (nyaris pecah): saya bikin produknya **berhenti
> mengambang** 3 cm di atas lantai. Justru pembekuan itu yang mengubahnya dari
> kecelakaan jadi efek. Versi yang benar ada di **Ide 22** di bawah: jatuh
> beneran, lalu ditangkap beneran. Ide sama, satu detail dibalik, hasilnya beda
> kelas.

**Aturan G — Retention by Action.**
Dua video dengan hook yang **persis sama**, hanya satu yang viral. Bedanya: yang
viral, si kreator **mengiris apel pakai pisau** sambil bicara. Yang gagal, cuma
talking head biasa.

> *"For example, pouring water into this glass. Your brain wants to see it spill,
> but it never does. That type of curiosity is what keeps people hooked."*

Jadi harus ada **aksi fisik biasa yang berjalan terus di bawah narasi**, dengan
tegangan kecil yang **tidak pernah tuntas**. Ini bukan hook — ini penahan
tontonan sepanjang video.

**Aturan H — Open loop, tegangan jangan diselesaikan cepat.**
Struktur dari transkrip ketiga:

```
hook → lead → body 1 → open loop 1 → body 2 → open loop 2 → body 3 → CTA
```

- **hook** mematahkan pola yang mereka lihat
- **lead** menarik ke detik 10 — *"begitu tertambat di situ, mereka tidak scroll"*
- tiap **body** memberi satu nilai, lalu **open loop** membuka rasa penasaran baru:
  *"tapi bukan itu yang paling gila…"*
- **CTA** mengambil penonton paling hangat ke langkah berikutnya

Cocok dengan kerangka 30 detik yang kamu kirim — cuma penamaannya beda.

---

## Konsekuensi strategis (penting)

Kalau hook terkuat itu **"mangkuk tergelincir"**, maka nilai AI kita **bukan
membuat yang spektakuler** — tapi **membuat rekaman biasa yang meyakinkan**.
Itu target yang lebih sulit tapi jauh lebih berharga: penjual yang tidak sempat
syuting tetap dapat 3 detik footage yang terlihat seperti kejadian nyata di
rumahnya sendiri.

Kelima ide di bawah semuanya: **tangan saja, tanpa VFX, tanpa hal mustahil.**

## Ide 21 — Merayap ke tepi meja · **GAGAL — terlalu standar, bukan AI hook**

**Cocok untuk:** semua produk · **Orang:** nol (tangan saja) · **Mode:** i2v · **Klip AI:** 5 dtk
**Mekanisme:** Retention by Action + tegangan yang tidak pernah tuntas.

```
Locked-off static camera on a tripod, eye-level and close to the surface of a wooden
table in an ordinary Indonesian home, warm daylight from the left. THE PRODUCT sits on
the table exactly as in the reference image, about 20cm from the table edge, which is
at the right of frame. Only hands and forearms are ever visible — no face, no body.

Beat 1 (0-1s): a hand is tidying the table, casually pushing a small stack of papers to
one side. The movement is ordinary and unremarkable. In doing so, the hand nudges the
product slightly closer to the table edge without noticing.

Beat 2 (1-2.5s): the hand keeps tidying other things, and each pass accidentally nudges
the product a little further toward the edge. The product is now half a hand-width from
falling. The hand does not notice and continues working elsewhere in frame.

Beat 3 (2.5-4s): the product is now overhanging the edge, balanced on maybe a third of
its base, tilting very slightly. The hand keeps tidying, still unaware. It does not
fall.

Beat 4 (4-5s): the hand finally moves back toward the product, and stops just short of
touching it.

FINAL FRAME: the product is balanced on the very edge of the table, overhanging and
tilted slightly, still not falling, sharp and centered, a hand paused just beside it.
```

**Negative:** `no face, no body, no head in frame, no falling product, no dropped product, no distorted hands, no extra fingers, no slow motion, no text`

| Waktu | Suara | Teks overlay |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | *"eh eh eh, awas—"* | **JANGAN SAMPE JATUH** |
| 2–5s | *"…tapi bukan ini yang mau aku tunjukin"* | — |

**Kenapa tidak terlihat AI:** tidak ada satu pun kejadian mustahil. Model cuma
diminta merender tangan merapikan meja — hal paling biasa.

## Ide 22 — Nyaris jatuh beneran · **GAGAL — terlalu standar, bukan AI hook**

**Cocok untuk:** semua produk · **Orang:** nol (tangan saja) · **Mode:** i2v · **Klip AI:** 4 dtk
**Mekanisme:** persis mekanisme "mangkuk tergelincir" yang dapat 25 juta views.

```
Locked-off static camera on a tripod, waist-level, framing a pair of hands over a hard
tiled floor in an ordinary Indonesian home, natural daylight. Only hands and forearms
are visible — no face, no body.

Beat 1 (0-0.8s): a hand holds THE PRODUCT casually at waist height, exactly as in the
reference image, turning it slightly as if about to show it to someone. Completely
ordinary.

Beat 2 (0.8-1.4s): the product slips out of the fingers and drops. Real gravity, real
speed, no slow motion.

Beat 3 (1.4-2.2s): the second hand darts in and fumbles it — the product bounces off
the fingertips once, tumbling, still falling.

Beat 4 (2.2-3.2s): both hands catch it awkwardly just above the floor, close to the
tiles. The hands are tense, fingers splayed from the effort.

Beat 5 (3.2-4s): the hands slowly straighten and bring the product back up to waist
height, holding it firmly now.

FINAL FRAME: both hands hold the product securely at waist height, centered and sharp,
undamaged and identical to the reference image.
```

**Negative:** `no face, no body, no slow motion, no freeze, no floating, no hovering, no broken product, no distorted hands, no extra fingers, no text`

| Waktu | Suara | Teks overlay |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | *"WOY—"* (refleks kaget, bukan akting) | **HAMPIR AJA** |
| 2–4s | *"…jantungan aku. tapi tunggu dulu"* | — |

> **`no slow motion, no freeze, no floating` itu wajib.** Tiga kata itu yang
> memisahkan ide ini dari Ide 16 yang gagal. Begitu benda berhenti di udara,
> penonton tahu itu buatan.

## Ide 23 — Kupas film pelindung · **GAGAL — terlalu standar, bukan AI hook**

**Cocok untuk:** gadget, elektronik, apa pun yang datang berlapis plastik · **Orang:** nol · **Mode:** i2v · **Klip AI:** 5 dtk
**Mekanisme:** Retention by Action murni — "apakah bakal sobek?" tidak pernah dijawab.

```
Locked-off static camera on a tripod, top-down close-up onto THE PRODUCT lying flat on
a plain wooden table in an ordinary Indonesian home, soft even daylight. The product
matches the reference image exactly and has a thin transparent protective film over its
surface. Only fingers and hands are visible — no face, no body.

Beat 1 (0-1s): a fingernail picks at the very corner of the protective film, catching
the edge on the second try.

Beat 2 (1-3s): the film peels back slowly and evenly across the surface, stretching
slightly, catching the light, making a long clean line. It is peeled about a third of
the way.

Beat 3 (3-4.2s): the film narrows and thins where it is stretched, as if it might tear
at any moment. The peeling slows right down. It does not tear.

Beat 4 (4.2-5s): the peeling stops with the film still half attached, hanging loose
over the edge of the product, still intact.

FINAL FRAME: the protective film hangs half-peeled off the product, unbroken, the
exposed surface underneath clean and sharp, product identical to the reference image.
```

**Negative:** `no face, no body, no torn film, no fully removed film, no scratches, no distorted hands, no extra fingers, no text, no logo`

| Waktu | Suara | Teks overlay |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | *"ini bagian yang paling nagih sih"* | **JANGAN SAMPE SOBEK** |
| 2–5s | *"tapi lihat pas dibuka semua nanti…"* | — |

## Ide 24 — Tumpukan yang goyang · **GAGAL — terlalu standar, bukan AI hook**

**Cocok untuk:** semua produk berkemasan kotak · **Orang:** nol · **Mode:** r2v · **Klip AI:** 5 dtk
**Mekanisme:** tegangan tak tuntas **plus** social proof (banyak stok) tanpa mengucapkan klaim apa pun.

```
Locked-off static camera on a tripod, low side angle onto a wooden table in an ordinary
Indonesian home, warm daylight. A tall stack of identical plain product boxes is
balanced on the table, about eight boxes high, leaning very slightly. All boxes match
the reference image product's packaging. Only hands and forearms are visible — no face,
no body.

Beat 1 (0-1s): a hand carefully places one more box on top of the stack. The whole stack
wobbles gently and settles.

Beat 2 (1-2.5s): the hand slowly lets go, fingers hovering close, ready to catch. The
stack leans a little further and keeps swaying slightly. It does not fall.

Beat 3 (2.5-4s): the hand withdraws fully out of frame. The stack sways on its own,
leaning further than seems possible, boxes shifting slightly against each other. Still
it does not fall.

Beat 4 (4-5s): the stack slowly settles into stillness, still leaning at an alarming
angle, intact.

FINAL FRAME: the tall leaning stack of boxes stands still and intact on the table,
sharp and centered, no hands in frame.
```

**Negative:** `no face, no body, no collapsing stack, no fallen boxes, no distorted hands, no extra fingers, no text on boxes, no logo, no slow motion`

| Waktu | Suara | Teks overlay |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | *"ini stok minggu ini doang loh"* | **JANGAN GOYANG** |
| 2–5s | *"…dan itu bukan bagian yang gila"* | — |

> **Rambu:** boks harus **polos tanpa tulisan**. Dan jangan ucapkan jumlah stok
> yang tidak benar — kalau stoknya tidak sebanyak itu, ganti kalimatnya jadi
> netral (mis. *"lagi beres-beres stok"*). Klaim stok palsu kena aturan L-13.

## Ide 25 — Kabel kusut yang diurai · **GAGAL — terlalu standar, bukan AI hook**

**Cocok untuk:** powerbank, charger, earphone, apa pun yang berkabel · **Orang:** nol · **Mode:** i2v · **Klip AI:** 5 dtk
**Mekanisme:** Retention by Action klasik. Otak menunggu simpulnya lepas.

```
Locked-off static camera on a tripod, top-down close-up onto a plain wooden table in an
ordinary Indonesian home, soft daylight. A short cable is knotted and tangled in a
messy ball in the centre of frame. THE PRODUCT sits just beside it, exactly as in the
reference image. Only hands and fingers are visible — no face, no body.

Beat 1 (0-1s): fingers pick at the tangled cable, pulling one loop through another. The
knot loosens slightly.

Beat 2 (1-2.5s): the fingers work steadily. One loop pulls free, then the knot tightens
again somewhere else. The tangle is smaller but still knotted.

Beat 3 (2.5-4s): the fingers slow down and work at one stubborn loop in the middle,
pulling it gently back and forth. It does not come free.

Beat 4 (4-5s): the fingers stop, leaving the cable still partly knotted, and rest
beside the product without picking it up.

FINAL FRAME: the cable lies still and partly untangled on the table next to the
product, which is sharp and centered, identical to the reference image.
```

**Negative:** `no face, no body, no fully untangled cable, no cut cable, no distorted hands, no extra fingers, no text, no logo`

| Waktu | Suara | Teks overlay |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | *"tiap hari gini terus, capek"* | **RELATE GAK SIH** |
| 2–5s | *"nah, ini yang akhirnya bikin aku berhenti ribet"* | — |

## Urutan uji

1. **Ide 22 (nyaris jatuh beneran)** — mekanisme persis video 25 juta views, cuma 4 detik, tangan saja, nol hal mustahil. Kalau satu ide harus dicoba, ini.
2. **Ide 21 (merayap ke tepi meja)** — tegangan paling panjang, paling murah dirender.
3. **Ide 25 (kabel kusut)** — paling relevan untuk powerbank yang sedang kamu tes.

## Uji pembanding yang sebenarnya paling berharga

Bikin **dua versi hook yang sama persis, beda satu variabel** — seperti dua
video "You don't want to study?" di referensi. Contoh: Ide 22 dengan tangan diam
vs Ide 22 dengan tangan yang sedang merapikan meja. Itu satu-satunya cara tahu
mana yang benar-benar menaikkan retensi, bukan menebak dari selera.
